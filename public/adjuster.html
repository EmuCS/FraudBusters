Claims queue

Every claim arrives with its evidence checks and reasons. Small claims that pass every check are paid automatically under your fast-track limit; everything else waits for an adjuster.

Fast-track rules

Claims at or under the limit are paid in seconds, but only when every check passes: live three-shot capture, unedited, never claimed before at any insurer, photos match the description, amount fits the damage, no recent claims. One warning sends the claim to the queue. Each rule is signed by your wallet and recorded on Solana.
